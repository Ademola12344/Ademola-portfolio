Project overview
Refined the homepage SEO for a watch ecommerce store targeting customers in the United States. The work focused on making the homepage metadata and on-page content clearer and more relevant to the intended search audience.

Work completed

  • Keyword research focused on the store’s products and US target audience.
  • Homepage title tag and meta description optimization.
  • Review and improvement of the main H1 heading.
  • Image alt-text review and optimization.

Scope
Homepage SEO refinement only. This case study does not claim a full website redesign, development, ranking increase or sales uplift.
